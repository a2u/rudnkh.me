A small fox with a sword, a cryptic in-game manual written in an invented
script, and a world that reveals a second, far deeper layer once you start
translating the runes. The first 10 hours feel like Zelda homage; the next
20 feel like the most clever ARG I've played in years.

Not for everyone — if "read the manual" isn't your idea of gameplay, skip.
For me, peak satisfaction.

Whenever I have some free time, I play the old Doom games. A new port,
[DOOM + DOOM II](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2280/DOOM__DOOM_II/),
was recently released on Steam, and it includes a community-made level
pack called [Master Levels for Doom II](https://doom.fandom.com/wiki/Master_Levels_for_Doom_II_(expansion_pack)).

I’ve never seen anything worse. I used to make better maps when I was
in school, and somehow this was officially released. Just look at
[this moment at 10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uhTU3pqd6NM&t=652s)
in `Vesperas.wad`. What kind of deranged level designer hides a key
required to finish the level inside a secret area? To reach it, you
have to follow an invisible path along a wall above a bottomless pit—the
mapper literally placed an invisible ledge only a few pixels wide there.

I have no idea how anyone managed to finish this level in 1995. At least
today we have hundreds of forum threads and YouTube videos where we can
find a hint.

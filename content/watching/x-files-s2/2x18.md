A standalone episode, unrelated to the main mythology arc. Mulder
and Scully work a case of alien abduction — except this time the
abductees are zoo animals, not people.

One thing that stood out: this is a 1994 episode, and there's a scene
of Mulder on a video call with the Lone Gunmen. In 1994. Technology.

The title is lifted from William Blake's "The Tyger":

> Tyger! Tyger! burning bright  
> In the forests of the night,  
> What immortal hand or eye  
> Could frame thy fearful symmetry?

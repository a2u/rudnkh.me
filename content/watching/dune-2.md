Villeneuve translating Herbert into pure cinema. Scale, sound, IMAX — all
of it carrying weight that most blockbusters fake. Hans Zimmer's score
does half the storytelling.

The political turn at the end is the part the marketing didn't sell, and
also the only reason the film lands.

Tatsuki Fujimoto adapting his own one-shot. 58 minutes about two girls
drawing manga and what happens when one of them stops. Animation by Studio
Durian — the kind of frame-by-frame craft you don't see at this scale
anymore.

The middle stretch on the bridge is the best sequence in any film I've
seen this year, anime or otherwise.

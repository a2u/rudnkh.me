The aesthetic is doing an incredible amount of work. Every frame is
composed like a Wes Anderson cover of a Kafka short story. The plot moves
slower than it thinks it does, but the atmosphere carries it.

The "innies vs outies" ethics keep finding new dimensions. I suspect the
show is structurally incapable of landing a finale that matches the
premise, but the ride is the point.

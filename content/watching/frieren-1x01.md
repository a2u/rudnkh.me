Premise: the hero's party defeats the demon king on page one, then the
elven mage spends the rest of her long life thinking about the fifty
years they spent together. A meditation on grief disguised as fantasy.

The pilot does its job — sets the elegiac tone without becoming maudlin,
trusts the viewer to fill in the silences. Madhouse's animation is
restrained on purpose; the show doesn't reach for spectacle until it
matters.

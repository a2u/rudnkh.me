Outer Wilds

2026-02-18

Finally played Outer Wilds. It lives up to the reputation — probably the purest expression of curiosity-as-gameplay I’ve ever seen. No combat, no upgrades, no gear checks. Just you, a ship, and a solar system full of things that deserve to be understood.

Spoilers would genuinely hurt the experience, so I’ll just say: every “aha” moment lands, and the structure of the final act is audacious.

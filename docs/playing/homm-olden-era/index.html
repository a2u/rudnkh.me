Heroes of Might and Magic: Olden Era

2 May 2026 · released 2026 · store ↗ · igdb ↗

I wouldn’t call myself a Heroes of Might and Magic fan. But as someone who enjoys turn-based strategy and isometric games, I couldn’t pass on this release. Played through it in early access (only Act 1 of the campaign was available at the time).

The story campaign is short — I finished it in a single day. The plot is mostly there to have one; nothing memorable. And mechanically, I didn’t see anything substantively new compared to earlier entries in the series — honestly, it feels more like a remaster than a new game.

Andor, Season 2

2026-03-11

The best thing Star Wars has done in decades. Tony Gilroy writing adult political drama with grown-up pacing and stakes. The prison arc in S1 was already a small miracle; S2 keeps the bar there for twelve episodes.

Watch even if you don’t care about Star Wars. Especially if you don’t.
